About OPS Academy

What is OPS Academy?

OPS Academy is the training and education arm of Knotty Opulence. OPS stands for Operational Precision and Skill. Our hands on training connects the cultural and spiritual traditions of textured hair care with practical techniques you can use for yourself, your family, and your community. Ancestral crownkeeping. Practical skills. Portable legacy.

Why learn crownkeeping?

Crownkeeping is knowledge that travels. It lets you preserve and pass down ancestral approaches to crown care, care for your own hair and your family's hair, and carry those skills wherever you live. For some students, it is also a foundation for further education or income, subject to the laws and licensing requirements where they work.

Why choose OPS Academy instead of cosmetology school?

Because this knowledge was ours long before anyone issued a license for it. Braiding, locking, and caring for textured hair are ancestral practices, carried in our hands and passed down through generations of Black Women. Over time, much of that wisdom was repackaged, renamed, and sold back to us, along with the belief that we needed someone else's permission to practice it.

At OPS Academy, we teach crownkeeping as it was always meant to be held: as a transferable skill, an ancestral practice, and a legacy you can pass on. Whether you are enrolling yourself or your child, you are investing in a Black Woman's vision and in a living lineage of knowledge, not in an industry's definition of who we are.

Cosmetology school prepares you for a license. OPS Academy prepares you to carry the craft. For some students the two paths work together, and we will always tell you plainly what your goals may require where you live.

Certificates and certification

What is the difference between a certificate and the Certification?

A certificate of completion shows you finished a specific class and the skills it covers. The OPS Academy Certification is our complete training program: 136 hours across 18 weeks, taught in a set order, with tuition, an enrollment packet, and a full assessment of your work. Classes are a great way to begin. The Certification is for students ready to make this their career.

Is a certificate or the Certification a cosmetology license?

No. OPS Academy is not a cosmetology licensure program. We teach transferable skills, not professional licensure. Certificates and the Certification are issued by OPS Academy by Knotty Opulence and reflect your training with us, not a government recognized license. If you plan to offer paid services, check the licensing requirements where you live and work.

Do the classes count toward the Certification?

Classes are a strong foundation and a good way to learn how we teach, but the Certification is its own program with its own curriculum and enrollment. Questions about your path? Message us and we will help you choose.

How do I enroll in the Certification?

Join the OPS Academy interest list. You will be the first to receive the enrollment packet with tuition, schedule, requirements, and next steps.

Choosing a class

Do I need experience?

Not for Level 1. Dads Do Hair and Wash Day, Done Right are made for beginners. Foundations of the Crown teaches braiding from the ground up. Braiding for Business is for braiders who already have the skills, and Beginner to Boss starts from the very beginning.

Do I have to take the classes in order?

No. Every class builds on the last, but you can start where you are. If you are not sure which level fits, message us and tell us your goal.

Are classes in person or virtual?

Dads Do Hair and Wash Day, Done Right are in person in Mérida. Foundations of the Crown, both Locs classes, Braiding for Business, and Beginner to Boss are available in Mérida or virtually.

Which classes are one on one?

Foundations of the Crown and both Locs classes are private, one on one with your instructor. Dads Do Hair and Wash Day, Done Right are small groups of up to 10.

Can I bring my child to Dads Do Hair?

Yes, that is the point. Each Dad attends with one child, ages 4 to 12. Children under 4 can join with approval on WhatsApp. Bring a tablet or quiet activity for breaks.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

All class payments are final and are not refunded. If you need to change your plans, we offer rescheduling and class credit instead, as long as you give notice. Credits are valid for 6 months toward any OPS Academy class.

What if I need to miss a group workshop?

Give us at least 72 hours notice and you can move to a future date or receive a full class credit. With less than 72 hours notice, or if you do not attend, your seat is forfeited, but you can still pick up your starter kit and products. You are always welcome to send someone in your place: just message us their name before class.

How do deposits work for private classes?

A $100 USD deposit holds your private class and is applied to your total. The deposit is not refundable, because your instructor sets aside private time just for you.

Can I reschedule a private session?

Yes. Reschedule at no cost with at least 48 hours notice. With less than 48 hours notice, or if you do not attend, that session counts as used. If you need to step away before your first session, your payment beyond the deposit becomes class credit. Once your program begins, you may pause once and finish within 3 months.

What if you cancel, or the weather does?

If we need to cancel or reschedule your class, you choose: a full refund or a new date. For events outside anyone's control, such as storms, hurricane season, or emergencies, we reschedule your class at no cost to you.

Are virtual classes recorded?

Most virtual classes are recorded, and the recording is sent to you after class so you can review at your own pace. If you miss a live session, you will still receive the recording when one is available, but live participation, questions, and feedback in that session cannot be made up.

What if my internet goes out during a virtual class?

If the connection fails on your side, the session counts as attended, and you will receive the recording when one is available. If it fails on ours, we reschedule at no cost to you.

After class

Is there support after I finish?

Yes. Ongoing mentorship is available for Braiding for Business and Beginner to Boss, and apprenticeship or chair rental is currently available for qualified graduates of the Maintenance Practitioner and Braiding for Business classes.
