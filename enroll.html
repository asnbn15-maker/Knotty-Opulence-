01 Welcome

Welcome to the founding cohort of the OPS Academy Certification. You are one of only three students chosen to begin this work with me in January 2027.

OPS Academy is the training and education arm of Knotty Opulence. OPS stands for Operational Precision and Skill. Here, crownkeeping is taught as it was always meant to be held: as an ancestral practice, a practical skill, and a legacy you can carry anywhere.

This knowledge was ours long before anyone issued a license for it. Braiding, locking, and caring for textured hair live in our hands and have been passed down through generations of Black Women. When you learn here, you invest in a Black Woman's vision and in a living lineage of knowledge.

I have been doing hair since I was 14 and have been licensed since 2011. For more than 20 years, my hands have learned what textured hair needs. Now it is time to pass that on.

With love and intention,
Nay, Founder, Knotty Opulence and OPS Academy

03 What this program is, and is not

The OPS Academy Certification is our complete training program in crownkeeping, taught in a set order and finished with a full assessment of your work.

What it is

  • Professional training, practiced on mannequins and on other people's textured hair: natural hair, children's hair, extension braiding, sew ins, and locs
  • An ancestral practice, taught with its cultural and spiritual roots intact and without assuming a shared belief
  • A transferable skill and a foundation for further education or income, subject to the laws where you work
  • A complete program on its own: no class is required before you enroll

What it is not

  • OPS Academy is not a cosmetology licensure program. We teach transferable skills, not professional licensure.
  • The Certification is issued by OPS Academy by Knotty Opulence. It is not a government recognized license.
  • If you plan to offer paid services, you are responsible for checking the licensing requirements where you live and work.

Classes and the Academy: our individual classes are for students who want to learn one skill without committing to 18 weeks. They end with a certificate of completion and do not count toward Certification hours. If you have taken a class and then enroll in the Academy, you may be eligible for a credit toward your tuition. Ask us when you enroll.

04 How training works

You train 3 days a week, up to 4 hours a day, for 18 weeks. Every skill moves from demonstration to mannequin practice before you work on a live model.

  1. Learn: context and theory for each technique, and why it matters.
  2. Watch: Nay demonstrates slowly, from more than one angle, and shows common errors.
  3. Practice: guided mannequin work with one correction at a time.
  4. Model work: supervised work on adult and child models, only after you pass the safety gate.
  5. Assess: knowledge checks and practical assessments throughout, with a capstone in week 18.

Your training week

Day 1Context and theory, first demonstration, guided practice, reflection and cleanup
Day 2Review, second demonstration, practice lab, feedback and cleanup
Day 3Third demonstration, applied practice or assessment, critique, records and sanitation

You record every training day in your Crown Keeper Training Planner, and Nay signs it. Client observation is recorded separately and does not count toward your 216 scheduled hours.

Working with models: mannequin work always comes first. Live model work begins only after you pass the safety gate: hand hygiene, tool sanitation, consultation and consent, tension awareness, and knowing when to stop. Child models require guardian consent, the child's own agreement, direct supervision, and breaks.

08 Attendance, cancellations, and recordings

All tuition payments are final. Refunds are given only if OPS Academy cancels.

Attendance and assessment

  • To earn your Certification, complete at least 90% of the 216 scheduled hours, pass knowledge checks at 80%, pass practical assessments at 85%, and pass every critical safety criterion.
  • Any critical safety failure pauses your live model work until you are retaught and reassessed.
  • Reschedule a session at no cost with at least 48 hours notice. With less notice, or if you do not attend, that session counts as used.

If you need to withdraw

  • Tuition is not refunded, and the $500 deposit is not refundable.
  • You may transfer once to the next cohort and continue where you left off.

If we cancel

  • If OPS Academy cancels or reschedules a session, you choose a full refund for that session or a new date.
  • For events outside anyone's control, such as storms, hurricane season, or emergencies, the session is rescheduled at no cost to you.

Recordings

  • Most lessons are recorded and shared with you to review at your own pace.
  • A recording does not replace hands on hours, and live questions and feedback from a missed session cannot be made up.
